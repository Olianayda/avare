In a press conference Sept. 30 at World Dairy Expo, U.S. Secretary of Agriculture Brooke Rollins announced the upcoming enrollment period for the 2027 Dairy Margin Coverage (DMC) program. The enrollment period will remain open through Dec. 18. The DMC program was established and has been revised to help dairy operations manage risk associated with the volatile milk and feed markets, provide a safety net for income stabilization and offer flexibility for producers to seek coverage that aligns with their overall risk management plan. “Putting farmers first means making sure dairy producers have practical tools to protect their operations when markets fluctuate,” said Farm Service Agency (FSA) Administrator Bill Beam in a press release. “Dairy Margin Coverage provides an affordable layer of protection when margins tighten, helping producers manage risk and keep their operations on solid ground.” Advertisement The Working Families Tax Cut Act reauthorized the DMC program through 2031, strengthening the program to provide dairy operations with greater risk protection. The following provisions to the program took effect in 2026 with the act and will continue in the year ahead:
Established production history based on the highest annual milk production level from any one of the 2021, 2022 or 2023 calendar years
Clarification on how to establish production history for new operations, those that began marketing milk after Jan. 1, 2023
Eligibility for operations to enroll their first 6 million pounds of production at the Tier I level
Operations having the opportunity to make a one-time election coverage level and coverage percentage, reducing premium costs by 25%
For 2027 enrollment, dairy producers are able to select DMC coverage levels ranging from $4 to $9.50 per hundredweight (cwt) and may also purchase higher levels of coverage for additional protection against risk. Producers who previously elected for that multiyear program option for 2026-31 must still certify to commercially market milk, sign a DMC contract and pay the $100 administration fee for each program year. To determine the appropriate level of DMC coverage for a specific dairy operation, producers can use the online dairy decision tool . 2027 projections As of Oct. 2, margin predictions for 2027 are weak. From January through September, the DMC margin is projected to stay below the $9.50 per cwt coverage level as milk prices remain in the $20-per-cwt range and feed costs approach $12 per cwt. However, forecasts currently indicate only Tier I milk production will be affected with the lowest margin forecast at $8.57 per cwt in July 2027. It is not until October 2027 that an indemnity payment is not expected with the DMC rising to $9.73 per cwt. If forecasts hold, the 2027 average DMC margin is projected at $9.34 per cwt. Remember, markets will change. More information about enrollment can be found on the FSA website or by visiting a local FSA county office .
