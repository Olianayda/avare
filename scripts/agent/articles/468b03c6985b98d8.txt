One of the most common questions I am asked related to automated milking systems is whether a farm should feed cows in the automated milking stall. This question comes from farms looking to adopt the technology as well as those looking to increase profitability in their current automated milking systems. To answer this question for your farm, we must consider how various factors – including system type (guided flow or free flow), pen design (freestall or bedded pack), stall design, bedding type and nutrition strategies – interact and influence each other.
