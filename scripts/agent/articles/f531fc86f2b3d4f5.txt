Source: USDA Milk Production report, Aug. 21, 2026 Cow numbers higher July 2026 U.S. cow numbers were estimated at 9.71 million head, up 199,000 from a year earlier. The trend is similar in the 24 major dairy states, where July 2026 cow numbers were estimated at 9.267 million, also up 185,000 head from July 2025 ( Table 1 ). Compared to a year earlier, 19 states had more cows than July one year ago; four states had fewer cows. Kansas and Wisconsin led all states in year-over-year growth, up a combined 59,000 head in July. That was partially offset by a combined 9,000-head reduction in Washington and Pennsylvania. Vermont was unchanged. Advertisement Milk output per cow higher The national average in monthly milk output per cow increased in July 2026 and was up 3 pounds from July 2025. Among major states, the average year-to-year change was up 5 pounds from the same month a year earlier. Affected by regional weather factors, variations in feed costs and income margins, the difference in output per cow among those states was wide ( Table 2 ). Georgia showed per-cow output increased 75 pounds per month compared to the same month a year ago, followed by a 20-pound increase in Pennsylvania, a 15-pound increase in Arizona and Texas; and a 10-pound increase in Florida, Idaho, Iowa, Minnesota, New York and Wisconsin. Milk production higher The preliminary estimate of overall July 2026 milk production was higher than the same month a year earlier. Nineteen states boosted production a combined 472 million pounds; three states reduced production a combined 40 million pounds. Year-over-year growth leaders were Wisconsin (up 72 million pounds), Texas (up 68 million pounds) and Kansas (up 66 million pounds). The states posting largest volume declines were California (down 28 million pounds) and Washington (down 10 million pounds). Kansas was the milk percentage growth leader for July 2026, up 15.42% from July 2025, with Oregon following at 6.82% and Florida at 5.85%. July 2026 production was down 2.04% from a year earlier in Washington and 0.8% in California.
