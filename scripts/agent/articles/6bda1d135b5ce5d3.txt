Metaphylaxis remains a widely used health management tool in U.S. feedlots, helping reduce the impact of respiratory disease in high-risk cattle. However, rising treatment costs, concerns about adding handling stress and increasing scrutiny of antibiotic use and antimicrobial resistance continue to apply pressure to identify and use alternative approaches. Health monitoring technologies show promise targeting animals needing intervention, but they remain insufficiently validated for broad adoption, leaving metaphylaxis the leading strategy.
