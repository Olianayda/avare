In June, Sen. Peter Welch (D-Vermont) and cosponsor Sen. Bernie Sanders (I-Vermont) introduced the Milk from Family Dairies Act of 2026 (S. 4906), which has since been referred to the Senate Ag Committee for consideration. The bill aims to improve market transparency, stabilize the dairy market, better align supply with domestic demand, and provide investment and incentives to rebuild regional dairy infrastructure, revitalize rural communities, and maintain consumer access to reliable and reasonably priced dairy products.
