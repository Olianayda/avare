Running a farming operation, you often run into difficulties, and in many cases, you know whom to call. If a piece of equipment breaks down, you know a mechanic to call when it’s something you cannot handle on your own. If livestock develop issues, you probably know the veterinarian to call to handle the issue quickly. But what happens if you run into a legal dispute? You may know an attorney in the community, but does that attorney practice in the area you have a dispute in? If you know an attorney or not, you should always take a minute to interview them to make sure they can handle the case.
