Dairy Margin Coverage Not only was June’s Dairy Margin Coverage (DMC) margin the highest DMC margin of 2026 so far, but it also marked the highest margin since August 2025 when the margin above feed costs reached $11.52 per hundredweight (cwt). Both the all-milk price and feed costs declined in June, but it was the weight of the lower feed costs that boosted the month’s Dairy Margin Coverage (DMC) margin. The market dynamics gave way to a DMC margin of $10.88 per cwt, $1.38 per cwt above the Tier I indemnity payment trigger point. (Read: Lower feed costs raise June DMC margin to $10.88 per cwt ) Advertisement At the time of this writing, the July margin is forecast to drop more than $1 from June to $9.62 per cwt. If realized, this margin remains above the Tier I indemnity trigger point, but only by a slim 12 cents, influenced by an all-milk price $1.18 lower than June’s at $19.92 per cwt. Feed costs are expected to rise in corn and soybean meal as dairy-quality alfalfa hay falls $9 per ton, resulting in a feed cost forecast of $10.30 per cwt, up 8 cents from the previous month. The actual July DMC margin will be announced Aug. 31 following the release of the USDA Agricultural Prices report. As of Aug. 26, market conditions are expected to deteriorate for the remainder of 2026 with DMC indemnity payments anticipated from August through December, mostly influenced by rising feed costs and lower all-milk prices. If markets remain, the smallest DMC margin will be realized in September at $8.23 per cwt, triggering indemnity payments in the Tier I category at the $8.50 per cwt coverage level. The lower DMC margin forecasts have moved the year’s average DMC margin forecast to $9.28 per cwt, influenced by an average all-milk price of $19.96 per cwt and an average feed cost of $10.68 per cwt. Dairy Revenue Protection Dairy producers managing risk through Dairy Revenue Protection (Dairy-RP) are eligible to cover revenue quarterly, up to five nearby quarters. In September, Dairy-RP coverage is available for the fourth quarter of 2026 (October through December), sign-up ending Sept. 15, through the fourth quarter of 2027. The market changes daily and Dairy-RP endorsements must be purchased between the Chicago Mercantile Exchange (CME) market closing and the next CME opening. Dairy-RP is also not available on days when applicable futures contracts move limit-up or limit-down, or on days when CME trading is closed due to holidays. Also, typically Dairy-RP coverage cannot be purchased on days when major USDA dairy reports that could impact markets are released. This includes Milk Production, Cold Storage and Dairy Products reports (see Calendar ). Estimated results for Dairy-RP participation in the second quarter were calculated by HighGround Dairy using the announced class and component prices and milk yields reports from the USDA before official results were announced by the USDA Risk Management Agency (RMA). Approximately 33% of the U.S. milk supply – or 19.9 billion pounds of milk – was covered under Dairy-RP during the second quarter, making it the second-largest total for the program since the third quarter of 2020, according to HighGround Dairy. An estimated 10 cents per cwt were issued as indemnities, and after factoring producer premium costs, the estimated net to producers was a loss of 24 cents per cwt. The evaluation of the program also revealed that if ignoring yield adjustment factors, only Class III would have triggered indemnities during this quarter as Class III settled below the 95% coverage level 62% of the time. Livestock Gross Margin for Dairy Livestock Gross Margin for Dairy (LGM-Dairy) provides protection when feed costs rise or milk prices drop, and can be tailored to any size farm. The program uses futures prices for corn, soybean meal and milk to determine the expected gross margin and actual gross margin. LGM-Dairy is similar to buying both a call option to limit higher feed costs and a put option to set a floor on milk prices. Coverage for LGM-Dairy can be purchased on expected milk marketings over a rolling 11-month insurance period. So the coverage period during September includes the months of October 2026 through August 2027. Sales periods for the LGM-Dairy program are open on a weekly basis each Thursday except when Milk Production and Cold Storage reports are released on the same day. Sales are also closed on biannual USDA Cattle Inventory report release days in January and July. Livestock Risk Protection The Livestock Risk Protection (LRP) program is a valuable tool for dairy producers as beef-on-dairy and strategic culling decisions are key parts of herd management and business decisions. For dairy producers, LRP coverage is available as LRP-Feeder Cattle (beef-on-dairy calves) and LRP-Fed Cattle (cull cows) with four additional options to select the appropriate coverage, including head count, targeted marketing weight, and coverage and length and level. No more than 12,000 head can be covered in a specific coverage endorsement with an annual limit of 25,000 head per farmer, per crop year (July 1 to June 30). The sales period for LRP coverage is open each afternoon after futures prices are settled and closes the following morning. Similar to Dairy-RP, LRP is not available on days when CME trading is closed due to holidays or when major USDA reports impacting prices are released, namely Cattle on Feed and biannual USDA Cattle Inventory. The USDA RMA also has the right to close sales at their discretion. Production and price outlooks
