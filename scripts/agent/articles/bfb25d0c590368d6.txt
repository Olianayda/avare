I am my parents' “why child.” It didn’t matter if I was being told that the sky was blue or that I needed to clean my room. I was always going to ask why. There were times when my mom took offense to this, but she and my dad eventually realized I wasn’t challenging their authority, I just wanted to understand their reasoning. While toddler me would nod and either ask more questions or go do as I was told, teenage me sometimes suggested alternative plans or ideas. Did I deliver some of these ideas rather impertinently? Absolutely, but sometimes my idea was actually a good one. Jenna Hurty-Person Editor / Progressive Dairy Email Jenna Hurty-Person
