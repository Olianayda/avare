Communication is a big deal. It is how we come to understand one another. It allows us to discover how another person feels, thinks, believes and dreams. Through communication, we gain insight into another person's goals, expectations, struggles, disappointments and victories. It gives us a window into the hurts they carry and the joys they celebrate. Without communication, we are left making assumptions, and assumptions rarely strengthen relationships.
