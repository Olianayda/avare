Home &raquo; Progressive Dairy &raquo; [Podcast] Stacy Rethman finds her place on the family farm Podcasts [Podcast] Stacy Rethman finds her place on the family farm October 6, 2026 Stacy Rethman joins guest host Audrey Schmitz in this episode of the Progressive Dairy Podcast to discuss how she returned to her family’s dairy, Rottinghaus Holstein Farm in Seneca, Kansas. After teaching middle school science, she now helps manage their herd while educating consumers about dairy farming through outreach and social media.
