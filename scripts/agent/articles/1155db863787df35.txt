It was the first week of my freshman year of high school. I was sitting in the second row of what I assumed was going to be my favorite class. It was Mr. Aplanalp’s ag class, and big things were afoot. For as long as I could remember, at that stage of my life, one of the things that I most looked forward to was donning that gorgeous blue corduroy jacket and becoming a member of the South Summit FFA chapter. Now, it was finally a reality. Although I was intimidated by and mostly terrified of the upperclassmen, I felt I was on the verge of something. While I felt far from becoming a big deal myself, I was quite sure that I was at least now part of something that was a big deal.
