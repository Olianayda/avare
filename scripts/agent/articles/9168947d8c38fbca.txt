Home &raquo; Progressive Dairy &raquo; Apply for Progressive Dairy's 2027 editorial internship program People Apply for Progressive Dairy's 2027 editorial internship program October 8, 2026 Related Apply for Progressive Publishing's 2017 editorial internship program Early applications open for Progressive Publishing's 2025 editorial internship program Progressive Dairy seeks editorial internship applicants Agricultural students with training in journalism or communications are invited to apply for Progressive Publishing's 2027 summer editorial internship program. The deadline for application consideration is Dec. 14, 2026. Internship responsibilities Editorial interns will contribute by writing feature and news articles for one or more of Progressive Publishing's agricultural magazines: Progressive Dairy , Progressive Dairy – Canada and Ag Proud – Idaho . Interns will also engage in copy editing, photography and other aspects of producing both digital and print content. The program can be customized to fit the intern’s specific skills, including design, web content creation, social media management and marketing. Eligibility requirements
Academic performance:  Applicants should have a GPA of 3.0 or higher.
Writing experience:  Previous writing experience is highly valued.
Academic standing:  Preference is given to students who have completed their junior or senior year coursework. Graduating seniors are also encouraged to apply.
Agricultural background:  While a background in dairy or agriculture is not required, it is preferred. Applicants with experience and knowledge of the industry will have an advantage.
Internship details
Location:  The internship is based at Progressive Publishing's headquarters in Jerome, Idaho, but strong remote candidates will be considered.
Compensation:  This is a paid internship.
Housing and transportation:  Assistance in finding housing and arranging transportation will be provided.
Duration:  Internships typically last eight to 10 weeks, with flexible start and end dates to accommodate academic schedules.
Application materials Applicants should submit the following for priority consideration by Dec. 14, 2026: Advertisement
Cover letter
Resume
Three references
Examples of published work relevant to their expertise
Class assignment materials are acceptable if published work is not available. For more information or to submit materials, please contact Progressive Publishing by email .
