High-oleic soybeans (HOSB) have quickly gone from a low rumble in the back of the crowd to taking their place center stage. With this, each week brings new discussion regarding HOSB – rightfully so as there are several considerations from nutrient variability to roasting to particle size. And although this feed can offer a wide range of benefits, including ration savings in certain cases, placement in the ration can also be a bit of a balancing act, and one filled with many acronyms, at that. So, with all the buzz around these beans, let’s start spilling the beans on key considerations for this feed.
