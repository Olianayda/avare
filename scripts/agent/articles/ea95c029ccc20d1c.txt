Home &raquo; Blogs &raquo; Progressive Dairy Just Dropping By &raquo; Just Dropping By: My kind of candidate Just Dropping By: My kind of candidate Yevet Crandell Tenney August 25, 2026 Here we go again. The news cycle and the mailboxes fill up with politics. Some of it’s true, and some of it's hogwash, as the old-timers used to call it. In case some of you weren’t raised with a pigpen in the backyard, that’s the stickiest, dirtiest place on the whole ranch. The pigs wallow in it to get themselves cool. It’s a good description of those who want to disguise their true natures. They want to appear clean, but they are never dirtier. Yevet Crandell Tenney Yevet Crandell Tenney is a Christian columnist who loves American values and traditions. She writ... Politics is the art of telling people what they want to hear. Truth is telling it like it was, is and eventually will become – no hemming and hawing or beating around the bush. No excuses. No bluffing. Just the plain unairbrushed truth.  A leader should not be a cardboard figure painted by the media. Not a makeover to sell the gig. Just themselves, plain and simple. He or she should be able to say, my journey is an open book for all to read. I stand for right and justice every time. I don’t fear the false witnesses with their lie-weaving wagging tongues. They will spin their lies in tiny half-truths and spread them with gnawing sound bites, but truth seekers will always uncover the lie. The Lord is my judge, and I face Him every night and every morning. My right hand knows what my left hand does. My heart and head are connected by a firm desire to serve my God. My actions are in full-blown daylight. No dark shadow rooms with sleazy deals and backdoor promises. I surround myself with honest, good-hearted people who will labor side by side with me to save liberty, freedom and the Constitution. I am who I say I am, and I do what I say I will do. My voting record and my speeches are never at variance. Prove me, and you will find my integrity. Oh, to find a candidate like that. I have said and written that before. And here it is again: Where are they? Politicians are making promises they may intend to keep but have no plan in place to do so. They offer full-blown lies woven into tidbits of truths and excuses for deplorable past behavior. Don’t you wish you could see into the hearts of the politicians? Don’t you wish there was a way to tell who is telling the truth and who is fabricating a fairy tale? Advertisement Where is our Moses or a Joshua – maybe even a young David to fight our Goliaths? A Joseph sold into Egypt would be nice in these economic times because he had a direct channel always tuned in to God. And those fiery furnace guys – Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego – who refused to kneel before the idols of the king even under strict command and pain of death? Where is the Queen Esther to our impending doom? I would even be glad for a Daniel who trusted God even in the den of ferocious lions. These stalwarts of the Bible were not politically correct cowards. Their allegiance was to God and to Him alone. Where is our modern George Washington who refused to be king? He chose to be a president grandeur in his time. Where is our Lincoln who led us through one of the darkest times in our nation’s history? He said, “Character is like a tree and reputation like its shadow. The shadow is what we think of it; the tree is the real thing.” Lincoln was not a man to say one thing and do another. Lincoln is also often attributed as the one who said, “Nearly all men can stand adversity, but if you want to test a man's character, give him power.” Perhaps modern politicians think they are living in a one-dimensional box where people see only what they want them to see, or they think people are amnesiacs who can’t remember from one sound bite to the next. One would think, with cameras rolling and recordings of the past, surely, they would remember what they said yesterday, but do they? Maybe politicians have their audience figured right. They know we are not listening to the sound bites. We are on our devices playing fantasy football, engaging in our favorite game, watching the latest movie or just plain texting. To be fair, most are working two jobs just to make ends meet and pay taxes. We are too busy to pay attention to sound bites and little lies that change the Constitution incrementally, locking one more link in the chain around the neck of freedom. Politicians gamble that we are not paying attention. Maybe they are right. Not so. The American people are a slumbering dragon that is waking up. One sleepy green eye is focusing on the treasure of freedom that is being stolen by the moral pigmies in our government. The dragon’s hot breath is mounting inside, making ready for explosions of righteous indignation that will make War of the Worlds look like child’s play. Somewhere there is a Moses who will, through the power and grace of God, bring down the plagues of Egypt and part the Red Sea and allow the righteous to walk through the river of adversity on dry ground. Of course, the righteous must put God first and give homage to Christ who is the King and Savior of this land. One day, God will raise up a Joseph of Egypt to teach the followers of Christ how to prosper in uncertain economic times. He will teach us to put away our toys and focus on things that really matter: God, family, food, shelter and caring for the poor among us. He will teach us to forgive and bring together our broken and blind. He will motivate us to put on color blinders to see all people as children of God. We will see that there is power and protection in serving the God of this land. There will come Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego and Esther who will honor God despite political winds. They will honor God; God will honor them. His promises are sure. We have seen God fulfill His promise in the past, and we will see it in the future. The fiery furnace was not a fairy tale. It was a miracle given to honor those who honored God. If it happened then, it could happen today. In my day, I have seen God turn away fire from a town at the request of His servants. During the Rodeo-Chedeski fire, we heard story after story from those who stayed in my hometown as it was in the path of the raging forest fire. Each morning, the men in Heber would meet at the church and pray for the town and its people. These men would arm themselves with the power of God and then disperse to fight the fire. Miracles happened. One Sunday, the wind that was driving the fire down the canyon was breathlessly close to Heber. The smoke and flames rolled in closer. The men prayed and commanded in the name of the Savior, and miraculously, and almost on cue, the wind changed direction saving the town from devastation. In other instances, the fire burned right up to the porches of dwellings and churches then backed away. One prayerful woman recounted that the debris burned around her house and even under her big propane tank but left her home untouched. God honors those who honor Him. We see Daniels walk out safe from the lion’s den. These are ordinary people, but God makes them strong with His power. These work miracles in His name. He can likewise teach us to prosper. Maybe we are the ones. Maybe we are the Moses, Josephs and Esthers of the future. Maybe we will lead people across the Red Sea. Maybe we are the ones who will rise and call down the powers of Heaven. God is no respecter of persons, but he favors those who follow Him and keeps His commandments. Perhaps we are the spears that will wake the slumbering dragon to save our freedom from the politicians who have lost touch with the truth. We are the prayer warriors, and we can vote for those leaders who will champion the truth and the Constitution because God honors those who honor Him. He will honor people who lead in righteousness as He did Washington and Lincoln. God is the same yesterday, today and forever.
