“Over the years, the ranch has been many things in its lifetime,” says Dr. John Hall, University of Idaho (U of I) professor and superintendent of the Nancy M. Cummings Research, Extension and Education Center (NMCREEC) in Carmen, 8 miles north of Salmon. “We had a milking parlor when I first got here – someone had milked cows here. There were lambing jugs, so someone had sheep.” It has also done time as a cash hay operation, a purebred Hereford operation and – for the past 25 years – the primary cow-calf and forage research station for the University of Idaho.
