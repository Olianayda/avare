In the current cattle market, the greatest threat to many producers isn't a sudden price drop – it is the cost of inaction. Many operations treat risk management like a speculative trade, waiting for the perfect moment to capture the absolute peak of the market. In reality, effective cattle price risk management should be treated like a utility bill: a predictable, necessary cost of doing business that ensures the operation can keep its lights on. Waiting for an ideal market scenario leaves equity exposed to sudden, unforgiving downturns. Garren Bellis Business and Risk Management Consultant / Compass Ag Solutions Email Garren Bellis
