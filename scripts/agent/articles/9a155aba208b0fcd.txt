Beef cattle are raised across an incredible range of environments. Unlike other livestock species, which have largely removed the environmental variation from their systems, our cattle span the extremes of environments around the world. These environments present the full range of stressors that can impact productivity: from extreme heat and humidity, to brutal cold, to toxic fescue, to predation, to parasites and pathogens. This means that oftentimes, an animal’s ability to express its full genetic potential may be impaired by having to allocate resources toward dealing with another environmental stressor.
