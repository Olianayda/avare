Every story starts somewhere. Over the past 10 years, the Dairy Excellence Foundation has impacted 10 million lives and helped spark 10 million stories. From inner city students who have never set foot on a dairy farm to college students who gained practical herd management skills that helped lay the foundation for their farm-level careers, our programs have impacted 10 million students across the nation. Every story, big or small, matters as we work to inspire the dairy producers, consumers and advocates of tomorrow.
