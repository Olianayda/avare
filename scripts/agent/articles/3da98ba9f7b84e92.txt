Cover crops were originally used to keep bare fields from losing soil, inviting weeds or suffering soil surface drying –  the rapid moisture loss and heat buildup that weaken soil biology. Traditionally, these fast‑growing annuals were plowed under to add organic matter and improve soil health while protecting the surface from wind, sun and erosion. Today, many producers also graze cover crops, turning them into dual‑purpose forage that can be seeded early for fall grazing or planted in autumn to provide feed through winter and early spring.
