September 2026 new product rollout August 27, 2026 New Holland launches Speedrower 1 Series self-propelled windrowers New Holland has introduced the Speedrower 1 Series, a new generation of self‑propelled windrowers built for commercial hay and forage operations that need to cover more acres and protect crop quality in tight harvest windows. The four‑model lineup ranges from 161 to 308 peak horsepower, led by the Speedrower 301, and pairs higher power with upgraded controls, improved cooling performance and lifetime FieldOps connectivity. New Holland updates model year 2027 Roll-Belt 1 Series round balers New Holland North America is introducing model year 2027 updates to its Roll‑Belt 1 Series round balers, focusing on durability, net wrap performance, automation and clearer model naming. The upgrades include a new net protection system for 5X6 balers that uses electrohydraulic belt tension control at bale ejection to reduce net damage in heavy residue. Larger rollers, heavier components and more robust bearings have been added in key wear areas, including the tailgate, back wrap and follower rollers, to improve reliability. Kuhn Merge Maxx 1102 and 1302 commercial hay mergers Kuhn North America has introduced the next generation Merge Maxx MM 1102 and MM 1302 triple hay mergers, built for high‑volume commercial hay and dairy operations. The new models feature ISOBUS controls, a heavy‑duty 42‑inch belt and a redesigned solid floating wind guard that improves crop capture, reduces loss and forms uniform windrows for efficient chopper feeding. High‑capacity hydraulics and the wide‑belt crop flow design help the mergers perform smoothly in dense, heavy forage. Operator efficiency is strengthened through the IntelliMerge ISOBUS system, which centralizes machine functions and diagnostics in the cab. Commercial‑grade pickups with split bearings and anti‑wrap discs reduce wear and downtime, while spring belt tensioning and OptiSense belt stall detection help prevent plugs before they occur. Designed for uptime, ease of use and crop protection, the MM 1102 and MM 1302 deliver dependable performance in demanding conditions. The new mergers are now available through authorized Kuhn dealers across North America. For more information, visit the website . Image courtesy of Fliegl. Fliegl optimizes the TMK 200 S Jumbo heavy-duty dump trailer Fliegl has optimized its TMK 200 S Jumbo heavy‑duty dump trailer with updates that improve stability, durability and material flow – all relevant for forage, manure and biomass hauling. The new design removes the steering axle and adds two rigid BPW brake axles with 410-by-120-millimeter drum brakes, creating exceptional torsional rigidity when dumping on uneven ground. The integrated BPW walking‑beam suspension lowers the center of gravity, improving stability when unloading heavy, wet or sticky material often encountered in forage and manure systems. For transport between fields or from bunkers to storage sites, the hydraulically suspended, height‑adjustable drawbar absorbs shocks and maintains smooth towing. The dump body – built from high‑strength steel with HB 400 to 450 wear‑resistant floors – resists abrasion from silage, gravel or soil and maintains a clean interior for consistent unloading. Hot‑dip galvanized Profi frames add long‑term corrosion protection. Standard features include a hydraulic tailgate with gravel discharge, recessed tie‑downs, dual‑circuit air brakes with load‑sensing control and a hydraulic parking jack. Options such as larger tires for reduced compaction, upgraded hitches and camera systems allow producers to tailor the trailer for forage hauling, bunker work or manure transport. The TMK 200 S Jumbo is available through Fliegl dealers across North America. For more information, visit the website . Image courtesy of Mil‑Stak Manufacturing. Mil-Stak introduces new bale bundle attachment Mil‑Stak Manufacturing has introduced the Mil‑Stak Bale Bundle Attachment, giving hay growers and custom operators a faster, more efficient way to retrieve bundled small square bales from the field. Compatible with Mil‑Stak self‑propelled and pull‑type machines as well as newer StackCruiser models, the attachment handles bundles produced by Bale Baron, Bale Band‑It and Arcusin systems. By increasing the number of bundles moved per trip – up to 12 bundles with Mil‑Stak machines and up to 10 with compatible StackCruisers – the attachment helps operators reduce field passes, lower fuel use, limit soil compaction and shorten the time between baling and storage. This efficiency is especially valuable for growers serving horse markets, livestock producers, feed stores and export buyers who rely on mechanically handled small‑bale bundles. Designed to fit equipment many producers already own, the attachment provides a practical way to streamline small‑bale logistics without adding another dedicated field machine.  For more information, visit the website . AgroLiquid adds humic and fulvic products AgroLiquid has added six humic, fulvic and sugar-based products – acquired through its purchase of Monty’s Plant Food – to expand its crop nutrition portfolio. The new offerings give retailers and growers additional tools to improve nutrient efficiency, strengthen soil health and support higher yield potential. The lineup pairs Monty’s humic and fulvic technology with AgroLiquid’s high efficiency fertility programs to enhance nutrient availability, uptake and long-term soil productivity. The additions include Monty’s Liquid Carbon for improving soil structure and nutrient release, Surge XD for boosting nutrient uptake through plant tissue, Drivas as a pure fulvic additive, Agri Sweet FG to feed beneficial microbes, Dri Carbon for concentrated humic conditioning, and Premium Blend for stronger emergence and early vigor. Together, these products support more efficient nutrient use, healthier soils and improved crop response across a range of production systems. For more information, visit the website .
