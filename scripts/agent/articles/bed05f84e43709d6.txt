Email Audrey Schmitz The U.S. farm real estate value, a measurement of the value of all land and buildings on farms, averaged $4,500 per acre for 2026, up $150 per acre (3.4%) from 2025. The U.S. cropland value averaged $6,020 per acre, an increase of $190 per acre (3.3%) from the previous year. The U.S. pasture value averaged $2,000 per acre, an increase of $80 per acre (4.2%) from 2025. Dairy states 2026 cropland values among the 24 major dairy states rose only about 3.4% from 2025, a little more than the national average. Year-over-year increases of 4% or more were recorded in Florida, Indiana, Minnesota, Utah and Wisconsin. Smallest increases were recorded in Arizona, Idaho and Washington, each less than 2%.
