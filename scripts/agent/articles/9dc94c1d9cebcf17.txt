Every decision on today’s dairies can have an impact on animal health, welfare and beef quality. The 2026 Dairy Wellbeing Workshop, presented by Professional Dairy Producers (PDP), includes an exclusive beef processing plant tour for dairy producers and their teams to see firsthand how animal care choices impact beef quality. Each day, the tours will be followed up by sessions sharing the latest strategies shown to improve dairy herd health outcomes.
