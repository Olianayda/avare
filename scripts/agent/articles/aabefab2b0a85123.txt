Dairy Farmers of Canada: New proAction housing requirements coming in April 2027 September 1, 2026 Dairy Farmers of Canada (DFC) has finalized new requirements for the housing of calves, dry cattle and lactating cattle in the proAction Animal Care module. Related Dairy Farmers of Canada: New transportation of animals regulations coming into effect in February Dairy Farmers of Canada: New partnership with Dairy Farmers of Canada and Cineplex Media to promote real butter Dairy Farmers of Canada: An overview of new requirements for proAction The new requirements were developed based on the current scientific evidence demonstrating their benefits and align the module with the 2023 updates to the Code of Practice for the Care and Handling of Dairy Cattle. They will be part of on-farm validations starting in April 2027. All the information about the upcoming update will be available this October in the new proAction Reference Manual and Workbook. Farmers should contact their provincial organization when new versions of these documents are published. Farmers can learn more about how to prepare through the Farmer Resources section of the Dairy Farmers of Canada website, which is also the home of the new proAction website . As you prepare, look for the new Dashboard tool on the proAction website in October 2026. It will give farmers easy access to the requirements and enable them to test their readiness with a quiz before an on-farm validation. Advertisement The requirements were tested on a sample of farms across Canada, with feedback from participating farmers shaping the final requirements. At the 2026 DFC annual general meeting this past July, proAction Committee Chair Vicky Morrison led a panel on the upcoming proAction requirements, giving context to the reasoning behind these changes. She said that continuous improvement has always been one of the program’s foundations. “This evolution ensures relevance and credibility, but it also must be balanced with on-farm practicality,” she said. “We are confident that, even though this is a significant update, we've done our best to balance that necessity for continuous improvement and the incorporation of the Code of Practice with on-farm application.”
The Canadian Podcow talks about new housing requirements Looking for an overview of the upcoming requirements while you work in the barn? The Canadian Podcow has you covered. In the episode “New requirements for housing in proAction,” hosts and dairy farmers Sarah Sache and Andrew Campbell talk to University of Guelph professor Trevor Devries, co-chair of the Dairy Cattle Code of Practice Scientific Committee. They explore the new housing requirements in the Animal Care module, discuss how the Scientific Committee informed these requirements and what benefits farmers will see from this evolution of the program.  Listen to the episode, as well as the complete  Canadian Podcow catalogue, on your favourite podcasting platform now. Dairy Farmers of Canada (DFC) is the national policy, lobbying and promotional organization representing Canadian dairy producers. DFC strives to create stable conditions for the dairy sector in our country. It also seeks to maintain policies that promote the sustainability of Canadian dairy production and promote dairy products and their health benefits. Visit DFC’s  website  for more information.
