Two dairies start their breeding programs looking almost identical. Both run a timed-A.I. protocol for first service. Both breed nearly all their cows within a tight seven-day window. On paper, their first-service execution is a match. The difference often shows up after the first breeding: One farm quickly identifies open cows returning to heat and breeds them again, while the other waits for a pregnancy check and resynchronization. That gap makes return-heat detection one of the most important numbers to watch once first-service compliance is under control. Mark Doornink VP of Digital Technology / Parnell Email Mark Doornink
