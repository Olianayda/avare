But when a food safety issue makes headlines, it quickly becomes something everyone notices. When consumers hear about an outbreak or recall, they often do not distinguish between one company, one product or one facility, and some will just stop consuming that product altogether. Their confidence in the entire category can be affected, and this is why food safety is a priority for the dairy checkoff. Farmers and dairy companies are in the business of producing food that nourishes people, and customers expect it to be safe. So it is the industry’s responsibility to deliver on that expectation. We have seen major recalls or outbreaks in other food categories cause consumers to avoid an entire category, even when most companies had nothing to do with the problem. For dairy, a significant food safety incident can affect consumer confidence, sales and ultimately the financial health of companies and cooperatives – impacts that can work their way back to the farmer. Advertisement That is why we always say that food safety is not competitive. It is about protecting our consumers. Through the farmer-founded Innovation Center for U.S. Dairy, the checkoff has helped create a place where dairy companies, cooperatives, academics and other experts can come together around pre-competitive subjects that matter to the entire industry, such as food safety. Today, about 150 food safety professionals from across the dairy industry and academia volunteer their time to participate in this work. That includes leaders and experts from some of the country’s largest dairy companies, as well as smaller processors, and artisan/farmstead cheese and ice cream makers. These individuals bring decades of experience in sanitation, microbiology, plant design, pathogen monitoring and other areas. They may work for companies that compete every day, but when it comes to food safety, they recognize that sharing knowledge benefits everyone. The Innovation Center provides a neutral table where that sharing can happen. We do not tell a company what equipment to buy or promote one solution over another. Instead, we help identify what is working, bring the right experts together and turn that knowledge into resources the broader industry can use. Sometimes that means developing detailed guidance documents for a specific food safety challenge. Other times, it means training classes, online courses, webinars or simple tools that can help a smaller processor implement good practices without needing the resources of a large company. That last point is especially important. Our work is designed to help companies of all sizes with a lot of our efforts focused on small, growing companies. A small cheese or ice cream maker may not have a large quality department or a team of food safety specialists. But through the Innovation Center, that company can access guidance, training, templates and expertise developed with some of the best minds in the industry. Innovation Center companies fund the development of these resources, making them available to processors of all sizes. We also work with academic partners and help support research that addresses gaps in our collective knowledge. The goal is straightforward: To find better answers to food safety challenges and make those answers available to the industry. This work happens behind the scenes every day as the checkoff helps bring the right people to the table to set priorities and ensure the industry has the resources and knowledge it needs to protect the safety of our products. Our No. 1 priority always will be to protect consumers. When that happens, it allows us to help protect the reputation and future of dairy. When consumers lose confidence, everyone can feel the consequences. That is why food safety remains a shared responsibility and why the checkoff will continue convening industry leaders and other experts to solve problems that are bigger than any one company. And it will be done with the mindset that what is good for one is good for all. This column was written by Tim Stubbs, senior vice president of product research and food safety for Dairy Management Inc.
