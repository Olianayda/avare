Data released for lending rates in the second quarter of 2026 offered a look into the agricultural credit conditions of major agricultural regions across the U.S. Reports from Chicago, Dallas, Kansas City and Minneapolis Federal Reserve districts revealed interest rates for most variable-rate loans were relatively consistent with the previous quarter as the interest rates for fixed-rate loans mostly saw a slight increase over the same time period.
