The American Dairy XPO (ADX) will bring producer-focused education to Syracuse, New York, Nov. 4-5, with the ADX Dairy Classroom addressing some of the dairy industry’s most important challenges and opportunities. Sessions will explore succession planning, work-life balance, sustainability and Scope 3 expectations, artificial intelligence and the future of dairy.
