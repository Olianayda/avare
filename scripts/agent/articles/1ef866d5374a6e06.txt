A vegetarian and a carnivore walk into a bar … Gus Brackett Freelance Writer Gus Brackett lives and works on his family ranch in Three Creek, Idaho, where they raise cattle, horses and kids (little humans, not little goats). He is married to a saint and they have three wonderful children … and the other one. His family wishes to remain anonymous because they are certain they will be the subject of one of these columns, and they want to see if the column is any good before they put their name on it. The ranch is marginally profitable, so don’t worry about their well-being. He’s not trying to support a family with his writing.
