QualiTru Sampling Systems appoints Keira Lombardo as board chair September 23, 2026 QualiTru Sampling Systems , a leader in the science of aseptic and representative sampling equipment for the liquid food and dairy industries, announced on Sept. 17 the appointment of Keira L. Lombardo as chair of its board of directors. Related QualiTru Sampling Systems appoints Carmen L. Becker to board of directors QualiTru Sampling Systems launches customized food safety and quality training program for commercial dairy industry Idaho cow-calf and stocker producer Kim Brackett to serve as Cattlemen's Beef Board chairwoman Lombardo brings extensive executive leadership and board experience across the food, dairy and protein industries, with particular expertise in enterprise strategy, growth, organizational transformation and governance. She currently serves as chief strategy officer of Cal-Maine Foods Inc., the nation’s largest egg producer and a leading egg-based food company. “Keira brings an exceptional combination of strategic thinking, food industry experience and board leadership to QualiTru,” said David Roesser, Ph.D., CEO of QualiTru Sampling Systems. “She understands how to help organizations grow and evolve while building on the people, culture and capabilities that made them successful. That perspective, coupled with her experience navigating growth and transformation across the food industry, will be invaluable as we consider the opportunities ahead for QualiTru.” We are grateful for Bruce Faulken’s many years of service as a trusted legal adviser, counsel and former board chair, and for the guidance and perspective that have helped shape the company’s success. His guidance, wisdom and deep understanding of the company have been invaluable in shaping its growth and success. Before joining Cal-Maine Foods, Lombardo served as CEO of Dairy MAX and spent two decades with Smithfield Foods, ultimately serving as chief administrative officer and a member of its executive leadership team. Across these roles, she has worked closely with boards and leadership teams on strategy, growth, governance and organizational transformation. She also serves on boards spanning food manufacturing and technology.
