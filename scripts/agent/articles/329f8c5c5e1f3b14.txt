Who knew that what started as having to come up with a prefix for my first homebred Jersey would grow into such a brand and new business ventures for me. Back in 2018, I purchased my first Jersey through an online sale in Wisconsin. A year later, when she calved, I came up with the Lavender Ridge Jerseys prefix for the start of my Jersey herd. As the years have passed, this prefix is now on several animals, but it has also worked its way into my farm stand business as well.  Julianne McCormick Dairy Producer / Freelance Writer Julianne McCormick is a freelance writer in Ohio and a member of the Dairy Girl Network.
