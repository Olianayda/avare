For more than four decades, the World Forage Analysis Superbowl held during World Dairy Expo has shaped forage management across the dairy industry. What started as a hay and haylage contest has evolved into one of the most influential educational platforms in modern forage production – an event where producers, agronomists, nutritionists and seed company staff come together to compare, question and learn from the best forage samples in North America.
