Livestock operations are critical to the community, but farms must work hard to achieve effective operations with safety and efficiency. Outdated or poorly designed handling facilities can increase stress on the animals, leading to illness, injuries or risks to worker safety. Proper design can help to keep livestock in better condition whether they are entering the facility, moving to a different area for treatment or leaving. With this guide, livestock producers will learn some of the key design considerations for livestock-handling and housing facilities.
