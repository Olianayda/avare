MorningBio Mathistin MorningBio unveils Mathistin, an innovative rumen-protected L-histidine designed to optimize dairy performance. Manufactured with joint matrix-coating technology, Mathistin protects L-histidine from ruminal degradation and enables efficient release and absorption in the small intestine. CowManager Milk Sensor CowManager is expanding its smart cow monitoring platform with the development of a milk sensor designed to provide producers with actionable insights for earlier mastitis detection and improved milking performance. Delivering precise, real-time insights at both the cow and parlor level, the milk sensor monitors individual cows at each quarter liner. This added visibility helps producers identify potential mastitis earlier while also providing valuable insights into milking equipment and milker performance to support milk quality and operational efficiency. The milk sensor adds another layer of actionable insight to CowManager’s connected herd management platform, complementing existing monitoring solutions for health, fertility, nutrition, transition and youngstock. CowManager is taking the first steps toward introducing the milk sensor in 2026, with broader availability planned for 2027. Sign up for updates and availability details. Booth: #2217 Exhibition Hall Courtesy image. Feed Supervisor System Supervisor Systems recently launched a significant update to their feeding management software. Included in this update is the weather collection feature, automatically pulling online local weather data and correlating it with your feed records to reveal seasonal trends and how they affect energy-corrected milk (ECM) and dry matter intake (DMI). Manage your feeding program with ECM and utilize numerous new graphs for a clear visual of your herd’s performance. The weather feature helps you see how temperature and humidity influence DMI. By linking weather patterns to actual DMI data, you can adjust rations proactively, reduce shrink and maintain more consistent intakes. This data also supports better ECM results: Matching feed delivery and formulation to weather-driven intake changes helps protect milk components and overall production efficiency. You gain clear insight into the weather-intake-performance relationship, enabling confident, data-driven decisions year-round. Booth: #2127 Exhibition Hall Courtesy image. GEA DCMini GEA’s new DCMini brings clean-in-place pipeline wash control, cooling, agitation and operational functions together in a compact, economical package. It is designed for small- to medium-sized dairies and is ideal for retrofit projects, handling up to four tanks or silos with one control unit. The DCMini features a 10-inch horizontal touchscreen with the flexibility to add sensor packages and expand capabilities as your dairy’s needs evolve. DCMini gives you visibility into key wash performance indicators, including temperature, flow and conductivity. Cloud-based historical data makes it easy to review past washes, production and loadout information, so farms can make quicker decisions, improve accountability and identify issues before they become larger milk quality concerns. Built-in wiring diagrams and interactive diagnostics also help simplify troubleshooting.
