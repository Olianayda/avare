The U.S. Dairy Education and Training Consortium (USDETC) van slides into park, sending a host of students reaching over each other to pull on their bright green safety vests and stretchy rubber shoe covers. Screeching open, each van door slides to reveal 10 students eager to begin their day. Some students step onto a dairy for the first time, and the smell of milk, cattle feed and silage wafts into their nose instantly.
