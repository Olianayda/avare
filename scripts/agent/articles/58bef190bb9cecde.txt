Minerals are essential for growth and reproduction in beef cattle. Trace minerals can be offered to cattle in a number of ways: injectable, loose free-choice, trace mineral-fortified salt block or energy/protein trace mineral-fortified supplement. Each method has its own advantages and disadvantages in administration. Having forages analyzed for trace mineral concentrations can help when determining which trace minerals and how much are needed in a supplementation program. Analyzing forages for trace minerals can provide information to limit possible issues with trace mineral deficiencies.
