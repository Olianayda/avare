It was the first week in June, way too early for fire season, and I was under no small amount of stress. A lightning-caused fire, courtesy of Mother Nature’s Memorial Day fireworks, was working its way into its third week. Depending on the wind at any given time, the fire had alternately raged and inched its way across my entire Forest Service grazing allotment. For myriad reasons, I was living on the uneasy edge of anxiety. At the same time, though, I was counting my blessings.
