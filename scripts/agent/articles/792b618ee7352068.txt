Although he didn’t get the Golden Boot and Norway didn’t make it past the quarterfinals, in some ways, Erling Haaland still won the World Cup. In addition to being one of the highest-paid soccer players in the world, the 26-year-old striker is known for his humor and good nature, and not surprisingly garnered a lot of attention during the competition. His seven goals were impressive, including the two that knocked out perennial contenders Brazil. However, it was the number of memes that followed Haaland around that might have been even more remarkable, from imitating the way he runs to finding household objects that resemble his long hair and unique build.
