October 2026 new product rollout September 24, 2026 Kuhn North America introduces the Kuhn GMD 15030 rear-mounted quad disc mower Kuhn North America has introduced the GMD 15030 rear‑mounted quad disc mower, now the largest mounted disc mower available. John Deere introduces hands-free baling solution John Deere has introduced hands-free baling, an integrated automation system designed to simplify and standardize baling cornstalks and straw. The coordinated solution combines multiple John Deere technologies, including AutoTrac guidance, AutoTrac Turn Automation, gate automation, speed automation and weave automation on a 1 Series round baler. Together, these systems allow the tractor and baler to automatically follow windrows, weave for square-shouldered bale formation, manage tractor speed, control the baler gate and execute repeatable headland turns. The goal is to reduce manual inputs, improve consistency and help operators of all experience levels complete baling efficiently during tight harvest windows. Internal testing shows the system can increase productivity by up to 8.4%, improve fuel efficiency by up to 4.4% and reduce operator activity by as much as 76%. By automating steering and baler control functions, hands-free baling helps operators stay productive longer while freeing time to focus on other priorities across the operation. The system also extends the value of existing precision ag investments by using guidance lines created during earlier corn or wheat passes and improving documentation through the John Deere Operations Center. Hands-free baling is available on select 1 Series round balers paired with 6R, 7R and 8R tractors equipped with the required precision technologies. For more information, visit the website . Case IH expands Nutri-Tiller 1000 Series with new 16-row configuration Case IH has expanded the Nutri‑Tiller 1000 Series with a new 16‑row folding mounted toolbar configuration and an added knife/coulter row‑unit option, giving producers more ways to increase productivity while maintaining consistent strip quality. Built on agronomic design principles, the Nutri‑Tiller 1000 Series create clean, precise strips that support strong emergence, efficient nutrient use and long‑term soil health. The system blends the benefits of conservation‑minded no‑till with the agronomic advantages of conventional tillage by leaving protective residue between rows while placing fertilizer in a concentrated zone within the strip to maximize nutrient availability. When paired with a 2000 series Early Riser planter, the strip‑till and planting system works together to improve planter ride quality, establish a uniform seedbed and support early, even emergence. Operators can adjust residue managers from the cab to maintain strip cleanliness and reduce the need for additional tillage passes. The Early Riser row unit delivers precise seed placement, consistent depth control and optimal seed‑to‑soil contact directly within the prepared strips. Case IH FieldOps and Active Implement Guidance extend this system approach by helping ensure the planter follows the exact strip‑till path. Guidance lines can be shared across the fleet, while implement drift is corrected in real time to maintain alignment, reduce crop damage and limit compaction. Kuhn North America introduces the Kuhn HR 6042 R power harrow Kuhn North America has introduced the HR 6042 R power harrow, a 19-foot, 6-inch machine designed to deliver consistent seedbed preparation with improved efficiency and operator convenience. Replacing the HR 6040 R, the updated model retains Kuhn’s proven driveline and rotor system while incorporating a redesigned frame that improves accessibility, reduces soil buildup and simplifies maintenance. The single‑arm, single‑pivot layout provides easier access to gearboxes and key service points, and the folding design enhances lifting, balance and transport stability by keeping the center of gravity closer to the tractor. Built for high‑horsepower operations, the HR 6042 R features a heavy‑duty, oil‑cooled central gearbox rated for up to 460 PTO horsepower. Its rotor and blade design creates uniform soil tilth at depths up to 10 inches, helping operators produce a high‑quality seedbed while reducing fuel use and limiting the number of passes required. In‑cab hydraulic control of working depth and leveling bar adjustments allows quick responses to changing soil conditions, and multiple rotor speeds and rear roller options support fine‑tuned finishing across diverse crops and soil types. The HR 6042 R serves specialty crop growers, row‑crop farms, contractors and turf managers needing precise soil conditioning and dependable seedbed results while maximizing productivity in demanding conditions. JCB launches new high-tech Fastrac 6000 Series tractors JCB has introduced the new Fastrac 6000 Series, delivering a major step forward in power, precision and productivity for farmers and contractors. The lineup includes two models, the 6260 and 6300, offering up to 335 horsepower and a redesigned chassis that improves strength, balance and operator comfort. The series adds multiple high‑performance upgrades, including extra heavy‑duty axles with advanced suspension, a new engine and powertrain combination for improved torque and fuel efficiency, and a central tire inflation system that optimizes pressures for field and road use. A key advancement is the integration of Twin Steer guidance, which coordinates steering through both axles for ultra‑precise implement tracking. New integrated twin GPS receivers support faster, more accurate line acquisition, while multiple headland guidance patterns help operators match different implements and field layouts. High‑capacity hydraulics, with a standard 205‑liter‑per‑minute pump and optional 410‑liter‑per‑minute system, increase compatibility with large, demanding implements. Additional updates include improved braking components, easier service access, new digital camera coverage, a secondary display option and configurable screens that allow operators to tailor information to their needs. With these enhancements, the Fastrac 6000 Series expands JCB’s high‑speed tractor concept into a new performance category, offering a versatile, highly productive machine for fieldwork and transport.
