Consider this scenario: Worker A completes all the required job training both at the farm and with online materials, goes back and reviews information, asks their supervisor questions to understand what is expected and tries to implement new skills learned in the training. Worker B attends the required on-farm training session, is somewhat inattentive, does not talk to the supervisor after the training and does not appear to use their new skills on the job. Both workers “checked the box” for training when the time came for review and pay raise decisions. Both workers received a similar pay increase. Given the high cost of labor, is this strategy likely to yield the highest return on your labor investment?
