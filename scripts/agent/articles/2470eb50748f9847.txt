For producers who depend on a baler during harvest, the middle of baling season is perhaps the worst possible time to find out that a baler belt needs to be replaced. Whether baling hay, grass or cornstalks, the priority during the season is keeping equipment running to avoid costly and preventable downtime. Yet baler belts are often overlooked because operators are focused on the overall machine, field conditions and the bales themselves.
